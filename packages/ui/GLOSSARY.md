# UI

Shared components that the apps use. Each component is free of app content.

## Language

### Pixel Loader

**Pixel Loader**:\
A 3×3 or 5×5 grid of cells that light up in a timed pattern to show that content is loading.\
_Avoid_: Spinner, pixel wave icon

**Pixel Pattern**:\
The order in which the cells of a Pixel Loader light up, such as ripple, radar, or rain.\
_Avoid_: Preset, wave

### Pixel effects

**Dia Colors**:\
The five colors of the pixel effects, from pink through orange and yellow to lime.\
_Avoid_: Rainbow, gradient

### Ascii Ocean

**Ascii Ocean**:\
A canvas that draws fish schools, Siren, and her mermaids as ASCII cells. It fills its parent and keeps every animal inside it.\
_Avoid_: Shark animation, aquarium

**School**:\
A group of fish of one species that swim together. A school forms a ribbon, a swarm, or a bait ball by its species.\
_Avoid_: Flock, swarm

**Siren**:\
The owner's character: a round, white seal mermaid with two hair buns and a red tail. She swims belly to the viewer, from spot to spot.\
_Avoid_: Mermaid, player

**Mermaid**:\
One of Siren's two small companions, a third of her length. A mermaid has a white head with a red patch and two antennae, and swims at her side.\
_Avoid_: Fish, pet

**Swimmer**:\
Siren or a mermaid. Fish part around a swimmer.\
_Avoid_: Character, sprite

**Lure**:\
The spot of a click. Siren swims to it for a short time.\
_Avoid_: Target, bait
