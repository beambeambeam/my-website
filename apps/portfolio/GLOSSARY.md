# Portfolio

The personal portfolio site at thisisbeam.fyi. It presents the owner, their work, and the tools they use.

## Tools

**Tool**: A language, library, framework, or product the owner works with a lot. Shown in the "I work a lot with" card. _Avoid_: Tech, skill, stack item

**Tool category**: A named group of tools, such as Languages or Backend.

**Mono logo**: The one-color shape of a tool's logo. The card shows it in the foreground color at rest. _Avoid_: Icon

**Full-color logo**: A tool's logo in its official brand colors. It replaces the mono logo on hover. _Avoid_: Colored icon

**Brand color**: The one main color of a tool. It colors the tool name on hover. It also colors the logo of a tool that is mono by design.
